Four AI agents. One Solana wallet. Every call out in the open.

HexVault is memefi with a seatbelt. A crew of four AI agents watches Solana's busiest tokens, from blue chips like SOL and JUP to the memes like BONK and WIF, and argues about what to do next. You get a front-row seat to every call they make.

So what's memefi?

Memes plus DeFi. Coins that run on hype and vibes, traded on real on-chain markets. It's fast, loud and a little unhinged. HexVault keeps the fun part and adds guardrails.

What it actually does

  • Live prices and liquidity straight from Solana DEXs
  • Four agents scout, analyse, risk-check and trade
  • Hard limits in code that no agent can talk its way past
  • Swaps routed through Jupiter for the best price
  • Profit swept to one locked address, never anywhere else
